## Research & Learn
### What are common cyber security threats in a remote work environment?
***Phishing Attacks***
- deceptive emails/messages/text/phone calls
- alerts designed to trick you into revealing login credentials
- downloading malware

***Unsecure Wi-Fi Networks***
- public or poorly secure home networks
- interception of data moving between devices and company servers

***Credential Stuffing***
- attackers using leaked passwords
- using the same username and passwords for multiple logins

***Device Theft***
- loose security around laptops and phones in public
- no passcodes to unlock devices

### What are best practices for keeping your devices and accounts secure?
- always lock your screen when stepping away from the device
- keep all software updated - to combat malware and viruses
- encrypt your storage - stops data being accessed when device is stolen

### Why is it important to lock your computer when away from your desk?
- any sensitive data will become accessible by anyone that can reach the computer
- you are responsible for your device - suspicious actions on your device will be blamed on you

### How should you handle phishing attempts and suspicious links?
- inspect the senders full email before opening - small differences matter
- hover over links before clicking - most browsers show the destination URL in the bottom corner
- verify using a different means - if you get a suspicious message on Slack, check via Discord or Email.
- report it - always and immediately
- never click on links in emails - type the address in the browser when you can

### What makes a strong password, and why should you use a password manager?
- use a strong password - long, unique, and random
- use a password manager - reduces reliance on memory, increases complexity of passwords, generated

### Why is two-factor authentication (2FA) important, and when should you enable it?
- 2FA - essential second layer of defense - even if password is stolen, they need a way to access the temporary code
- enable it everywhere that allows it
