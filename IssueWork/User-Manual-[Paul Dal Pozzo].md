## How I Work Best
### I do my best work when...
- I am well rested and had my morning coffee
- I am working on something exciting
- My ideas are heard - even if they are shut down
- I feel like I am making a difference
- I have a full belly
- I have a clear direction
### I struggle when...
- My sleep apnea plays up and I wake up with a headache
- I am hungry
- I haven't had a morning coffee
- I feel rushed or that my task doesn't have enough time allocated for me to do a good job
- I feel like people are being lazy. I will never suspect this off the bat. I base it off repeated habits that I observe.

## Communication Style
### I prefer messages to be...
- Short and direct. Ask me for help or guidance is fine as long as you are willing to put the effort in
- Initially by text/email/teams/WhatsApp. This allows me to finish my task or set my mind to a mentality for helping others
- If a matter gets too difficult to handle via the way suggested above, I am happy to do verbal communication. Either in person or video chat.
- I don't mind small talk, but I rather it doesn't get in the way of my work. Save it for a break, lunch or after work.
- When talking about work, try not to joke around too much. I don't like joking about deadlines and tasks because it can create confusion.
- Although, I like to joke around a lot when we are not working or talking about work. I know, I can be difficult.

### The best way to reach me is...
- Text, email, WhatsApp or phone call. I prefer not by phone, but will use it.
- I do not use Discord much

### If you need something urgent, do this...
- Just call me or come to me in person. That is a clear sign that it is urgent and needs my attention straight away.

- I can make sure every one understands the goals.

- People are honest. Sometimes I can seem too direct that it might offend people. Let me know and I will try to adjust. But, also understand that I am never trying to offend you when it comes to work, I am just trying to give you feedback or suggestions that you can explore.

### My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is...

## Feedback & Growth
### I like feedback to be... (e.g., direct, gentle, with examples, public/private)
- Direct. I do not care if it is kind or not if it is genuine feedback.
- Examples are good, but just saying 'it's bad' is not. Tell me why you think its bad or could be better.
- Often. I am happy for feedback to happen often. Especially if you have a connection to the task I am working on. I will never discourage passion.

### When I make a mistake, the best way to let me know is...
- Up to you. 
    - messages/text/emails are good for me to remember the feedback
    - verbally I can see your emotions and maybe sit with you to help pinpoint my mistake
    - in a meeting is fine

### I appreciate it when teammates...
- Present a new perspective on something, even if it something I am not working on. This is such an underrated thing, yet it allows everyone to improve.
- Don't hide things from me. Just come and tell me.
- Are kind to each other and offer each other help. Sure, focus on your own work, but helping someone for 5 minutes after a meeting might save them 2 hours. You don't even have to come up with a solution, just help them understand the issues they are having and maybe offer to review the work once it is complete.
