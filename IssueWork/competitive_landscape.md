# Issue 7
## Reflection
### What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?  
Focus Bear sets itself apart by combining the core features of multiple productivity apps into a single platform. While most tools focus strictly on either distraction blocking or habit tracking, Focus Bear handles both simultaneously. Crucially, it is the only app in this lineup that enforces automated micro-breaks. These hard-coded breaks are highly effective for breaking hyper-focus, helping users remember essential self-care tasks like drinking water or standing up.

### If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over competitors?  
Despite the strong visual appeal of competitors, Focus Bear remains the most practical choice for a busy, structured lifestyle for three main reasons:
1. **All-in-one functionality:** It eliminates the need to pay for and manage separate apps for distraction blocking and routine tracking.
2. **Guaranteed self-care:** The forced micro-breaks ensure that health and wellness routines are not ignored during periods of deep work.
3. **Multi-platform support:** It ensures deep, system-level focus on the PC during the work week while syncing habits across devices.
4. **Data Privacy:** Focus Bear has strict guidelines and rules about how it handles data.

### What's one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn't?  
- **Freedom** - perks with partners  
- **Cold Turkey** - daily/weekly scheduling  
- **Tiimo** - device widgets
- **Routinery** - heavy AI integration

### Based on your research, what's one improvement you think Focus Bear could make?  
#### Comprehensive analytics dashboard with distraction graphs
To break bad habits, users need to understand them; seeing exactly when they get distracted, for how long, and which apps trigger those lapses provides the awareness required to change behavior. Adding these insights alongside habit-completion metrics would allow users to track their focus improvements over time and prove the app's long-term value./

Focus Bear does have app tracking which is the first step to this feature. The next step is showing the user and showing the user progress over time. This will also highlight the advantages of using Focus Bear.

## Research Breakdown
### Freedom
- Core features: Offers recurring focus sessions, advanced scheduling, lock modes, block lists, and curated focus music.
- Pricing: Free tier available; premium is $3.33/month or a $99.50 lifetime license.
- Standout element: Includes "Freedom Perks" which grant users discounts on partner websites and productivity tools.
- Target audience: Built for general audience use to reduce overall technology dependence; does not explicitly target neurodivergence or include built-in break systems.

### Cold Turkey
- Core features: Advanced daily and weekly block scheduling, keyword blocking, search restrictions, and automated block triggers
- Pricing: Free tier available; Pro tier is a one-time payment of $59.00.
- Standout element: Features an intense lock system that requires a computer restart to lift a block early, alongside built-in distraction graphs.
- Target audience: Geared toward power users who need strict, un-bypassable desktop blocking and detailed productivity data.

### Tiimo
- Core features: Visual daily calendar planning, custom routines, activity tracking, and cross-device syncing.
- Pricing: Free tier available; premium costs $11.99/month, $54/year, or $120/year for a family plan.
- Standout element: Features a soft, highly customizable lavender color palette and a gamified trophy system designed to boost motivation.
- Target audience: Explicitly designed by and for neurodivergent individuals, focusing entirely on visual planning rather than hard app blocking.

### Routinery
- Core features: Structured routine tracking, habit forming systems, and mood logging.
- Pricing: Free tier available; premium costs $5/month, $36/year, or a $71.99 lifetime license.
- Standout element: Heavy AI integration that allows users to build, adjust, and interact with their routines using voice commands and text chats.
- Target audience: Designed for mobile-first users looking to automate the tedious parts of routine setup through conversational AI.
