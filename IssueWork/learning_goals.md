## Reflection
### What do you hope to learn from this internship?

While I do not plan to start my own company, understanding the organizational structure and chain of command will allow me to enhance my skills, become more independent, and be more helpful to others.

### What skills are you most excited to develop?  
As mentioned above, I am most excited to develop my skills in testing and analysis. These skills are often severely underrated, yet they are the most important competencies to have; especially in the age of AI. I also see this as an opportunity to practice my code reading and comprehension skills.
